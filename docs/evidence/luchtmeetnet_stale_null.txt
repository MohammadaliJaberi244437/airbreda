## 4. Why the flag must be sticky across runs (UPSERT_SQL)
## Each hourly run re-fetches the newest 50 hours. Replaying three consecutive pages with hours
## 0, 1, 2 frozen at 30.0: once hour 0 has left the page, hours 1 and 2 are a run of two and
## look fine on their own. flag_bad_readings alone would therefore unflag them one run later.
## page hours 0..49: flags of the frozen hours on this page alone = [True, True, True]
## page hours 1..50: flags of the frozen hours on this page alone = [False, False]
## page hours 2..51: flags of the frozen hours on this page alone = [False]
## After the three runs through the sticky upsert (an unchanged value keeps its flag, a revised
## value gets the fresh flag), the frozen hours in the table are:
##   2026-09-20T00:00:00+00:00  (30.0, True)
##   2026-09-20T01:00:00+00:00  (30.0, True)
##   2026-09-20T02:00:00+00:00  (30.0, True)

## UPSERT_SQL as deployed:
    INSERT INTO sensor_readings (station_id, timestamp, component, value, is_flagged)
    VALUES %s
    ON CONFLICT (station_id, timestamp, component)
    DO UPDATE SET
        value = EXCLUDED.value,
        is_flagged = CASE
            WHEN sensor_readings.value IS DISTINCT FROM EXCLUDED.value THEN EXCLUDED.is_flagged
            ELSE sensor_readings.is_flagged OR EXCLUDED.is_flagged
        END
